STATISTICAL ARTIFACT NOTE:
  The average t-stat change of +1.030 is NOT evidence of improvement.
  Removing ~47% of trades roughly halves the denominator of t = mean / (std / sqrt(n)).
  With fewer trades, standard error grows proportionally to 1/sqrt(n), which mechanically
  pushes t-statistics toward zero (less negative). The mean PnL per trade got WORSE
  (-30.89 -> -38.32/trade), confirming the filter is selecting the losing trades more
  precisely. The apparent t-stat improvement is an artifact of subsampling, not evidence
  of a real signal.

  VWAP directional filter does NOT resolve the reversal pattern.

  - The two motivating cases (ES Wed 16:00) got substantially worse under filtering
  - 0/17 slots crossed significance threshold
  - 7/17 slots showed improved mean PnL but none turned profitable
  - Hypothesis tested and rejected.
